### Risk Assessment Report for 2024 Noto Peninsula, Japan Earthquake

**Event Information:**
- **Location:** 2024 Noto Peninsula, Japan
- **Magnitude:** 7.5
- **Depth:** 10.0 km
- **Tsunami Warning:** Yes
- **Predicted High Impact Probability:** 1.00

---

#### 1. Executive Summary
The 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake, with a magnitude of 7.5 and a shallow depth of 10.0 km, presents an extreme risk to the region. The probability of high impact is assessed at 1.00, indicating a certainty of significant damage and potential casualties. A tsunami warning has been issued, further escalating the urgency of the situation. Immediate and coordinated emergency response actions are essential to mitigate the impact on the affected population.

#### 2. Risk Interpretation
The earthquake's magnitude and depth suggest that it will likely cause severe ground shaking and structural damage in the surrounding areas. The shallow depth increases the intensity of shaking felt at the surface. The issuance of a tsunami warning indicates a high likelihood of coastal flooding and additional hazards. Emergency response teams must prepare for potential infrastructure collapse, mass evacuations, and the need for medical assistance for injured individuals. The overall risk level is critically high, necessitating prompt action.

#### 3. Recommended Immediate Actions
- **Activate Emergency Response Protocols:** Mobilize local emergency services, including fire, medical, and police units, to prepare for immediate response.
- **Evacuate Coastal Areas:** Implement evacuation orders for residents in coastal regions at risk of tsunami inundation. Establish safe zones and ensure clear communication of evacuation routes.
- **Conduct Damage Assessments:** Deploy teams to assess structural integrity of buildings and infrastructure, prioritizing hospitals, schools, and shelters.
- **Establish Emergency Shelters:** Set up temporary shelters for displaced individuals, ensuring access to food, water, and medical care.
- **Public Communication:** Issue timely alerts and updates to the public regarding safety measures, evacuation procedures, and ongoing assessments.

#### 4. Policy Recommendations
- **Strengthen Building Codes:** Review and enhance building regulations to ensure structures can withstand seismic events, particularly in high-risk areas.
- **Tsunami Preparedness Programs:** Develop and implement community education programs on tsunami preparedness, including drills and awareness campaigns.
- **Emergency Response Training:** Conduct regular training exercises for emergency response teams to enhance coordination and effectiveness during disaster scenarios
